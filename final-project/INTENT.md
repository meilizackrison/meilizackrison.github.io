Who it’s for. One real or realistic person, and the question they’d ask you. “My roommate, a history major, who asked what I’d even do with a DH minor” beats “people curious about DH.”

My definition, first draft. Two to four sentences: what the Digital Humanities are, in your words, today. It’s allowed to change by Dec 12; your reflections page will say how.

Three examples. Three real DH projects, each with its link and one sentence on why it fits your definition and would matter to your reader. Open each link yourself.

“Done.” Eight to twelve lines you’ll check the finished site against, each one checkable with a yes or a no, and at least three about accessibility. “Is accessible” can’t be checked; “every image has alt text that fits its purpose” can.

Units. At least four from this term you’ll use, and where.

One risk. What’s most likely to go wrong, and what you’ll do if it does.
