## Intent brief

Who it’s for: My friend McKenzie who is in my major who asked me why I wanted to add Digital Humanities as a minor when it has seemingly little to do with the Editing/Publishing industry.

My definition: Digital Humanities uses technology to amplify and showcase art, writing, and other humanities fields. It is a museum's archive, an app that chooses a poem a day, a corpus of Calvin and Hobbes works, and any digital media that is ultimately celebrating and expressing some essential humanity in us. 

Three examples: first, a blog showcasing articles about writing and publishing in a readable fashion, because the internet has transformed newspapers and magazines which are a part of the humanities. second, a digital art gallery showcasing art I've done myself, because the internet allows people who would not see a work in person to view art from all over. third, a library's webpage with events and services listed, because the intenet helps promote community building by reaching broader audiences. 

Done: 
- does each image have understandable alt text?
- does it pass the w3c code validator?
- do all combinations of colors and text have proper contrast (4:5:1)?
- are all buttons at least 44x44 px? 
- does the page work at 200% zoom? 
- does each webpage have a navigation link back to the homepage? 
- does each webpage have a unique color palette and typography?
- is all the written copy edited to comply with CMOS?
- is each hyperlink descriptive instead of just "click me." 

Four examples: I am going to use grid in all of the pages in order to ensure that it works at 200% zoom, I will be using the images unit in the museum's archive, I will be using the color unit in all of the pages as I make accesible color palettes for each, and I will be using the box model as I put content in their own containers. 

One risk: the agent will put a piece of information in a div when an article or section will work better, so I'd better ensure I've planned out where my written copy will go and how it will fit with the page before I ask the agent to start building code.
