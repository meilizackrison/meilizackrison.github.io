Superman: The Animated Series

This cartoon is so fun! While not as popular as its predecessor, Batman: The Animated Series, I have gotten a lot of joy out of it. It aired in 1996, and I remember this: there is an episode where Bruce Wayne takes Lois Lane on a date, and there is an episode where Clark Kent fakes his death. The whole show rocks. I love it.
