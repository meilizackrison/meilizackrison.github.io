Why these fonts

  • Libre Baskerville

    This is the headline serif. It carries the magazine feel: classic, polished, and a little literary without getting too ornate or hard to read.

  • Manrope

    This is the body sans-serif. Its rounded shapes keep the page friendly and modern while staying clear and accessible for longer reading.

  • Heading sizes and rhythm

    H1 is the biggest gesture, H2 makes the sections feel organized, and H3 gives the page clear subheadings. That hierarchy keeps the reading flow steady and easy to follow.

  • Line height

    The main text uses a generous unitless line-height of 1.6 to make paragraphs easier to read and more comfortable at larger zoom levels.
