Fantasy High

Fantasy High starts the whole adventure with a school full of magic, monsters, and very dramatic teenagers. It balances comedy and heart while introducing the main ensemble that fans keep coming back to.

The Unsleeping City

The Unsleeping City turns a giant metropolis into a playground of secrets, weird jobs, and danger around every corner. The campaign leans into city energy, strange systems, and a constant sense of investigation.

Fantasy High: Sophomore Year

The second year raises the stakes while keeping the charm of the original cast intact. It brings familiar faces back with bigger problems, more personality, and even wilder school events.

A Crown of Candy

A Crown of Candy mixes sweetness with sharp-edged politics and strange magic. It turns the fantasy of a confectionery kingdom into a campaign full of temptation, loyalty, and chaos.

The Unsleeping City: Chapter II

Chapter II deepens the mysteries of the city and pushes its characters into bigger decisions. The adventure keeps the same weird, lively atmosphere but adds more pressure and new layers to the world.

A Starstruck Odyssey

A Starstruck Odyssey sends the cast across the stars with a mix of cosmic wonder and interpersonal chaos. The campaign loves big imaginative ideas while still making room for jokes, love, and deep feelings.

Neverafter

Neverafter reimagines fairy-tale stories as a place where danger and wit collide. It feels magical and theatrical at once, with a strong sense of storybook structure and emotional payoff.

Fantasy High: Junior Year

Fantasy High: Junior Year pushes the characters into larger, stranger conflicts without losing the warmth of the group. It feels like the campaign where old storylines, school drama, and ambition all start hitting critical mass.

Cloudward, Ho!

Cloudward, Ho! gives the cast a skybound setting full of wonder, danger, and oddball invention. The campaign turns the idea of “taking to the clouds” into a story that feels adventurous, playful, and unexpectedly emotional.

City Council of Darkness

City Council of Darkness brings bigger stakes and a more political energy to the table. The campaign thrives on tension, power struggles, and decisions that make the city itself feel like a living, shifting story.

Toylight

Toylight plays with imagination in a warm, playful way while still leaving room for danger and wonder. It feels like a bright and heartfelt adventure built around creativity, play, and the magic of stories themselves.
