Velvet Words

About this theme

Velvet Words is built for a literary magazine with a little sparkle and a lot of wit. The palette leans warm and grounded, like paper, ink, and a well-loved bookstore booth. It feels polished without becoming too trendy, which keeps the mood elegant and playful at once. Together, the colors read as thoughtful, feminine, and quietly creative.
