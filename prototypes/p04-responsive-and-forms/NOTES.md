# NOTES — <responsive and forms>

## Intent
A form anyone can fill in, on any screen down to 320px, that lands them on a thank-you page. Easy to fill in on a phone with one thumb. The form should be for people voting on their favorite DC superhero, with a section to fill in who they want to nominate for their favorite DC sidekick, as well as a name and email address so they can receive the results.
